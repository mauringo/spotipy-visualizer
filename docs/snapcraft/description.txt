Turn your Raspberry Pi touchscreen or Linux desktop into a dedicated Spotify
now playing display. See large album artwork, track details and playback
progress in a fullscreen browser view, with touch-friendly controls for your
active Spotify device.

* Play or pause, skip tracks, toggle shuffle, and save your favourite songs.
* Choose macOS-inspired or classic themes, with light and dark modes.
* Switch themes automatically on a schedule with your preferred timezone.
* Show a clock when playback is idle, or enable an optional rotation of stock
  quotes and Treasury yield data.
* Adjust appearance and idle-display settings from the browser.

The snap runs a background service and includes a desktop browser launcher.
Your configuration and Spotify authorization persist across snap refreshes.

Getting started:

1. Create a Spotify developer application and register this redirect URI:
   http://127.0.0.1:60123/auth/callback
2. Add your client ID, client secret and redirect URI to
   /var/snap/spotipy-visualizer/common/Flask/spotify.ini
3. Open Spotipy Visualizer from your applications menu, or visit
   http://127.0.0.1:60123 on the computer running the snap, then select
   Connect Spotify.
4. Start music on your Spotify phone, computer or speaker.

Requires an internet connection, a modern browser, a Spotify developer app
and a Spotify Premium account with access to that app. Music plays on your
existing Spotify device; Spotipy Visualizer displays playback information
and sends playback commands. It does not stream audio.

Setup instructions, including headless Raspberry Pi authorization:
https://github.com/mauringo/spotipy-visualizer#connect-spotify
