Idle display

Start on the clock immediately when no music is playing. After playback stops later, the idle display appears after the configured number of consecutive paused or stopped playback checks. All pages follow your existing light/dark theme.

Default: 3. Errors and Spotify cooldowns do not count. Timing depends on your Spotify polling interval; this does not add requests. Comma-separated, up to 12 symbols. Apple: AAPL · Nvidia: NVDA · Ouster: OUST.

The clock defaults to 30 seconds; stocks and rates each default to 10 seconds, with separate durations. Yahoo refreshes at startup when enabled, then every 30 minutes by default. All displays share the cache; Yahoo cooldowns can extend the wait. Quotes may be delayed. The rates page shows 2Y yield futures and 5Y/10Y/30Y Treasury yield indices, not the Federal Reserve policy rate.

Text size and display recovery

100% uses the current enlarged text. Text adapts to the screen; larger sizes may wrap on small displays. Checked every five seconds. Applies even when rotation is disabled. Network retries and API cooldowns are unchanged. A completely frozen browser still requires an external restart.
